Privacy Policy — Sangtinel

Last updated: 2026-06-19

Sangtinel (“the app”, “we”) is designed with privacy first. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

The short version

  • All your data stays on your device. Sangtinel does not have servers and does not upload, sell, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
  • You can export or permanently delete all your data at any time from within the app.

What data the app stores (locally only)

The following is stored only in the app’s local storage on your phone:

  • Profiles you create: name, age, role, average cycle length, logged cycle dates, optional likes/dislikes, custom notes, and any mood entries you log.
  • App settings: language, alert time, week start, default cycle length, daily-tip preference, and (if you enable it) your app-lock PIN.
  • Shopping list items you add.

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (e.g. share a PDF report, add events to your own calendar, or send a backup file) using your phone’s standard share options. At that point it is handled by the app or service you choose.

Permissions the app may request

  • Notifications — to deliver the cycle alerts and daily tips you enable.
  • Calendar — only if you use “Add to my calendar”; used solely to write cycle events into a calendar on your device. The app does not read your other calendar data for any other purpose.

Data sharing

We do not share data with third parties. Exports you initiate (PDF, calendar, backup file) go only where you send them.

Children

Sangtinel is intended for adults. It is not directed at children.

Consent & responsible use

Sangtinel is a supportive, educational tool. It should be used with the knowledge and consent of the person whose cycle is being tracked. It is not intended for covert monitoring or control of another person.

Your choices

  • Export backup — Settings → Data & backup.
  • Delete everything — Settings → Data & backup → Clear all data. This permanently removes all profiles and settings from your device.

Medical disclaimer

Sangtinel provides general wellness and educational information only. It is not medical advice and predictions are estimates. Consult a healthcare professional for medical concerns.
